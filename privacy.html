Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

The Curly Willow Eatery (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit our website.

Who we are

The Curly Willow Eatery is a restaurant located at 141 Pine Street, Collingwood, Ontario, L9Y 2P1. You can reach us by phone at 705-444-1562.

Information we collect

We may collect or have access to limited information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide by phone or in person — such as your name, phone number, reservation details, dietary notes, or event and catering requests when you contact us directly.
  • Website usage information — standard technical data that may be collected automatically by our hosting provider or content delivery services (for example, IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and approximate location).
  • Embedded and third-party services — our site may include maps, gift card purchasing, and social media links. Those providers may collect information according to their own policies.

Our website does not currently include an online contact form. We do not intentionally collect payment card details through this website.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Respond to reservation, catering, and general inquiries
  • Operate and improve our website and guest experience
  • Communicate about your visit, event, or request when you contact us
  • Comply with legal obligations where required

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website and embedded services may use cookies or similar technologies to help pages load correctly, remember preferences, or understand how the site is used. When you first visit, a notice at the bottom of the page allows you to acknowledge this use. You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how certain features work (for example, maps).

Third-party services

We may link to or embed services operated by others, including:

  • Google Maps for location and directions
  • Gift card services (Ackroo)
  • Facebook and Instagram
  • Website hosting, CDN, and related infrastructure providers

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them before using those features.

Links to other websites

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

Data retention

Information you share with us by phone or in person is kept only as long as needed for reservations, events, guest service, business records, or legal requirements.

Children’s privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children online through this site.

Your choices

If you have questions about information we may hold about you in connection with a reservation or inquiry, please contact us by phone. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of certain personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the website after changes means you acknowledge the updated policy.
